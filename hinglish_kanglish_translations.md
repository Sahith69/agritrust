# AgriTrust AI — Hinglish & Kanglish Conversational Guide
## बोलचाल की Hinglish एवं ಆಡುಮಾತಿನ Kanglish (Farmer-First Romanized Guide)

This document provides the complete, field-ready translation of AgriTrust AI into **Hinglish** (Romanized conversational Hindi) and **Kanglish** (Romanized conversational Kannada). 

These Romanized scripts reflect how progressive farmers, youth, and field extension workers communicate over WhatsApp, SMS, and modern mobile apps across North India and Karnataka.

---

## 📌 Guiding Style Rules

1. **Phonetic & Intuitive Spelling:** Standard SMS/chat spelling that any smartphone user can read smoothly without hesitation.
2. **Clear Agricultural Terminology:** Use everyday farm terms (*dhaan*, *adike*, *patti*, *yele*, *keeda*, *hula*, *spray*, *khatra*, *roga*).
3. **No Robot Tone:** Sounds like an experienced local farmer or a friendly KVK specialist speaking face-to-face.
4. **Safety Maintained:** Technical chemical names (*Tricyclazole 75 WP*, *Chlorpyriphos 20 EC*, *Mancozeb*, *Bordeaux mixture*) remain untouched.

## 2. 🛡️ Safety Guardrails, Rejection Cards & Disclaimers

### A. Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Guard (Blurry Photo, Wall, Non-Crop Image)
* **English:** `"This does not look like a close-up paddy, arecanut or apple photo. Retake a well-lit close-up of the affected leaf, trunk or nut."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Yeh photo dhaan, supari ya seb ki saaf aur paas se li gayi photo nahi lag rahi hai. Kripya achhi roshni me bimari wali patti, tane ya phal ke bilkul paas jaakar dobara photo kheencho."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Idu bhatta, adike athava apple na clear aada photo thara kaanta illa. Dayavittu olle light nalli, roga bandiro yele, kaanda athava adike hathira hogi innondu sala clear aagi photo thegeeri."**

---

### B. Conservative Abstain Guard (Confidence Below 70%)
* **English:** `"Diagnosis withheld (Low Confidence). The image is ambiguous or lighting is poor. Retake a clear close-up or consult your local KVK extension officer."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Bimari ka pakka pata nahi chal paya (bharosa 70% se kam hai). Photo dhundhli hai ya roshni kam hai. Galat andaza lagane se bachne ke liye app ruk gaya hai. Kripya saaf photo dobara kheencho ya apne KVK se salah lo."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Roga pakka aagi confirm aagilla (confidence 70% ginta kammi ide). Photo blur aagide athava light saalalla. Thappu guess maadodhu beda antha app nilliside. Dayavittu clear photo innondu sala thegeeri athava KVK officer na contact maadi."**

---

### C. Tungro Virus High-Stakes Warning (For Hispa & Downy Mildew Diagnoses)
* **English:** `"Stunted plants with yellow-orange leaves can also be Tungro virus (no cure). If so, consult your KVK."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Warning: Agar paudhe chhote (baune) reh gaye hain aur pattiyan peeli ya narangi pad rahi hain, toh yeh Tungro virus ho sakta hai (iska koi ilaaj nahi hai). Aisa dikhe toh turant apne KVK se contact karo."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Warning: Gida giddavaagi nithu, yele galu haladi-kithale colour ge thirugidre adhu Tungro virus irbodu (idhukke yavude medicine illa). Hange kaandhre thakshana nimma KVK officer na contact maadi."**

---

### D. Benchmark Audit Disclaimer (Paddy 0.67 vs 0.94 QWK)
* **English:** `"Independent-test QWK about 0.67 (twin-free, lesion classes); headline 0.94 is inflated."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Imandar test score: Asli khet ke test me score lagbhag 0.67 hai; market me bola jane wala 0.94 ka score badha-chadhakar dikhaya gaya hai."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Nija thapasana report: Real field test nalli score sumaru 0.67 ide; horagade heluva 0.94 score jaasthi maadi helidhu."**

## 3. 💬 Grounded Chat: Conversational Question & Answer Responses

### Q1: "What should I do first?" (Mujhe pehle kya karna chahiye? / Naanu modhalu yenu maadbeku?)

#### Case A: Treatable Disease
* **English:** `"Start with organic and cultural steps first: {organic_adv}"`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Sabse pehle organic aur gharelu upaay apnayein: {organic_adv}. Agar bimari bahut zyada badh jaye, tabhi KVK ki salah se chemical spray karein."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Modhalu organic mathu natural steps maadi: {organic_adv}. Roga thumba jaasthi aadhre maathra KVK helidha chemical spray maadi."**

#### Case B: Incurable Viral / Phytoplasma Disease (Tungro, Yellow Leaf Disease)
* **English:** `"Warning: {display_name} detected. No chemical cure exists. Manage insect vectors, remove infected plants, and consult your local KVK."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Alert: Fasal me {display_name} paya gaya hai. Iska koi chemical ilaaj nahi hai. Ras choosne wale keedon ko roko, bimar paudhon ko ukhaad kar khet se bahar phenk do, aur KVK se sampark karo."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Alert: Bele ge {display_name} bandide. Idhukke yavude chemical medicine illa. Roga haradiso keeta galanna control maadi, roga bandiro gida na kittu bisihaaki, mathu KVK na contact maadi."**

---

### Q2: "Is there a cure?" (Kya ye bimari theek ho sakti hai? / Ee roga vaasi aaguttha?)

#### Case A: Treatable Disease
* **English:** `"Yes, {display_name} can be managed. Start with cultural/organic steps. If severe, registered chemical options are available from your KVK."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Haan, {display_name} ko control kiya ja sakta hai. Shuru me organic aur safai ke steps lein. Zyada infection hone par KVK dwara batayi gayi register chemical medicine use karein."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Haudhu, {display_name} roga na control maadbahudu. Starting nalli organic steps maadi. Thumba jaasthi aadhre KVK recommend maadiro chemical spray use maadi."**

#### Case B: Incurable Viral / Phytoplasma Disease
* **English:** `"No chemical cure exists. {display_name} is incurable; remove infected plants and manage insect vectors."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Nahi, iska koi chemical ilaaj nahi hai. {display_name} ek lailaaj bimari hai; bimar paudhon ko ukhaad kar nasht karein aur keedon ko rokien."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Illa, idhukke yavude chemical cure illa. {display_name} vaasi aagadha roga; roga bandiro gida kittu haaki mathu insect galanna control maadi."**

---

### Q3: "When should I spray?" (Spray kab aur kaise karna chahiye? / Maddu yavaga spray maadbeku?)

* **English:** `"Weather alert (rule-based estimate, not a spraying schedule): {weather_alert}. Here, 'protective measure' means the preventive option advised by your local KVK. For mild symptoms, start with cultural and biological steps ({organic_adv}). Do not schedule calendar sprays; chemicals are strictly for severe outbreaks under KVK guidance."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Mausam alert (rule-based estimate hai — koi fixed timetable nahi): {weather_alert}. Yahan 'bachav' ka matlab aapke local KVK ki batayi gayi safety spray hai. Halke lakshan me pehle organic upaay karein ({organic_adv}). Bina soche-samjhe har hafte spray na karein; chemical spray sirf bimari zyada hone par hi karein."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Weather alert (rule-based estimate — fixed schedule alla): {weather_alert}. Illi 'bachav / safety' andre nimma KVK heliro precautionary spray. Swalpa roga idhre modhalu organic steps maadi ({organic_adv}). Sum sumne prathi vaara spray maadbedi; chemical spray thumba roga idhre maathra use maadi."**

---

### Q4: "Why are you unsure?" (App ko pakka bharosa kyon nahi hai? / App ge yaake pakka nambike illa?)

#### Confident Diagnosis (Confidence $\ge 70\%$)
* **English:** `"The model diagnosed this image with {conf:.1%} confidence. The app declines to diagnose below 70%. For Hispa and Downy Mildew, Tungro can look similar."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"App ne is photo ko {conf:.1%} bharose ke saath pehchana hai. Jab bharosa 70% se kam hota hai, tab app koi galat andaza lagane ke bajaye ruk jata hai. Dhyaan dein: Hispa aur Downy Mildew ke lakshan kabhi-kabhi Tungro virus jaise bhi dikhte hain."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"App ee photo na {conf:.1%} confidence indha identify maadide. Confidence 70% ginta kammi idhre app thappu guess maadalla, stop aagutthe. Gamanisi: Hispa mathu Downy Mildew symptoms kelavu sala Tungro virus tharanu kanbahudu."**

#### Abstained Diagnosis (Confidence $< 70\%$)
* **English:** `"Diagnosis was withheld because confidence ({conf:.1%}) fell below 70%. The app declines to diagnose below 70%. For Hispa and Downy Mildew, Tungro virus can look similar in the field."`
* **🗣️ Hinglish:**
  > **"Bimari ka naam nahi bataya gaya kyunki app ka bharosa ({conf:.1%}) 70% se kam reh gaya. Khet me Hispa ya Downy Mildew kabhi-kabhi Tungro virus jaisi lag sakti hai, isliye KVK se confirm karein."**
* **🗣️ Kanglish:**
  > **"Roga confirm aagilla yaakandre app confidence ({conf:.1%}) 70% ginta kammi ide. Thota nalli Hispa athava Downy Mildew kelavomme Tungro virus thara kanbahudu, adhukke KVK nalli check maadi."**
